          Example

          A Rookie-style puzzle has 3 different digits and feedback clues only. This one has 4 clues.

          1. 729Guess 7 2 9: No digit is correct.
          2. 682Guess 6 8 2: 1 digit is correct and in the right place.
          3. 159Guess 1 5 9: 1 digit is correct and in the right place.
          4. 421Guess 4 2 1: 2 digits are correct but in the wrong place.

          The first clue rules out 7, 2 and 9. In the fourth clue the 2 is already out, so the two correct digits are 4 and 1, and neither is where the guess put it: 4 is not first and 1 is not third. The third clue has one correct digit, in the right place, and its 9 is out. The 1 is in the code, so the 1 is that digit, and it is in the right place: first. That also means 5 is not in the code. The second clue has one correct digit, in the right place, and its 2 is out. The 6 cannot be first, because the 1 is there, and nothing in this clue is in a different place, so the 6 is not in the code. That leaves the 8, and it is second. The code starts 1 8, and the missing digit is the 4, third. The answer is 184.

          Illustrative numbers only: with base points of 100, a solve at the target time (the speed bonus is then 1.25) with one wrong guess (accuracy 0.92) and no hint scores 100 × 1.25 × 0.92 = 115 points.
